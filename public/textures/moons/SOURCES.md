# Moon maps

Global maps of the larger moons, resized to 2048 × 1024, re-centred on the prime meridian where
the source was centred on 180°, with a relief (normal) map derived from each image's fine
brightness. All from Wikimedia Commons.

The Cassini maps of Saturn's moons are enhanced-colour composites (infrared, green, ultraviolet);
they are desaturated here towards true colour (those moons are close to neutral grey; Iapetus keeps
more of its real reddish-brown dark side).

Voyager 2 imaged only the southern hemispheres of the Uranian moons and Triton (their north was in
winter darkness). Those unimaged regions are filled with the moon's own terrain, mirrored and
softened so they read as unknown rather than left black; they are not real imagery.

Titan keeps its hazy look (its surface is hidden by the atmosphere in visible light).
