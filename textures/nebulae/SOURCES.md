# Nebulae

Built by `scripts/build_nebulae.py` (colours matched to reference looks, sky background removed,
cloudy edges, RGBA WebP for premultiplied blending).

| File | Subject | Source | Credit / licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| cosmic-cliffs | "Cosmic Cliffs" in NGC 3324 (Carina) | ESA/Webb weic2205a | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI (CC BY 4.0) |
| mystic-mountain | "Mystic Mountain" in the Carina Nebula | ESA/Hubble heic1007a | NASA, ESA, M. Livio and the Hubble 20th Anniversary Team (STScI) (CC BY 4.0) |
| helix | The Helix Nebula (NGC 7293) | ESO eso1205a | ESO/VISTA/J. Emerson, acknowledgment: Cambridge Astronomical Survey Unit (CC BY 4.0) |
| orion-cloud, rosette-ring, crab-shell, veil-filaments, lagoon-lane, north-america | procedural | generated | Solaris |

The procedural figures are placed in the sky near nebulae of their kind, enlarged; the photographs
sit in their real directions (enlarged, and the two Carina images placed side by side).
